# Morada Product Vision

## Purpose

Morada is a housing platform designed initially for Brazilians living in Ireland. It reduces the fragmentation, uncertainty and scam risk involved in finding and advertising accommodation.

## Problem

People looking for accommodation in Ireland currently search across Facebook, WhatsApp, Instagram, Daft and other fragmented channels. Information quality varies, listings are inconsistent and trust is difficult to establish.

Advertisers also struggle to reach suitable tenants, housemates or successors efficiently.

## Positioning

> Morada is the practical, trustworthy way to find a place to live in Ireland.

Morada must avoid absolute safety claims. The product should communicate what was actually verified instead of implying that every transaction is risk-free.

## Initial market

- Geography: all of Ireland
- Primary community: Brazilians living in Ireland
- Access: open to other nationalities
- Languages: Portuguese and English

## Core marketplace

Demand:
- people looking for rooms, flats, apartments and houses

Supply:
- property owners
- tenants advertising a room or transfer opportunity
- real estate agencies
- property managers

## Core product loop

Seeker:

1. Create account and profile
2. Search and filter accommodation
3. Review a trusted listing
4. Favorite it
5. Start a conversation
6. Schedule a visit
7. Negotiate directly
8. Potentially close the accommodation

Advertiser:

1. Create account
2. Complete identity verification
3. Create listing
4. Submit supporting documents
5. Pass Morada moderation
6. Publish
7. Receive conversations
8. Schedule visits
9. Close listing with a reason

## Product principles

1. Trust must be explicit and explainable.
2. Private addresses and verification documents must never be public by default.
3. Verification of identity and verification of the right to advertise are separate concepts.
4. The product should reduce friction without creating false guarantees.
5. Mobile is the primary authenticated consumer experience.
6. Public web supports discovery, SEO and shared links.
7. Admin is a private web application.
8. Build incrementally from the existing repository; do not restart the product.

## Long-term vision

Morada starts as a trusted housing platform and may evolve into a broader trust and services network for Brazilians living in Ireland.

Potential future areas include accommodation exchange, housing-wanted posts, compatibility matching, verified ratings, translation, professional plans and additional services.
